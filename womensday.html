My girl,
Today, 8th March, the world celebrates women. I want to celebrate you — the woman who changed my entire idea of what love can feel like. Before you, love was just a word. With you, it became a place. A place where my heart can be soft and honest and a little scared and still completely safe. I’ve seen you on your brightest days — laughing, glowing, filling every corner with this crazy warm energy that makes everything lighter. I’ve also seen you on your heaviest days — tired eyes, shaky voice, carrying more than you should ever have to carry. And on both kinds of days, one thing has never changed: I have been in love with you. Not the filtered version of you. Not the “strong” version of you. The real you. I promise to keep learning how to give you all of that, better and better, for as long as I am allowed to love you. Today the world says “Happy Women’s Day” to every woman. I am saying it to one — to my girl. To the woman I admire, respect, desire, and adore. Happy Women’s Day, my love. And tomorrow? Tomorrow the world gets to celebrate the day you were born, and I get to celebrate the fact that somehow, in this huge world, you are mine.
Yours, hopelessly and completely — your boy ♡
